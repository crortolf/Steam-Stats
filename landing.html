Here you can find all kinds of interesting data related to you, your friends, and the games you all love. In order to use this site, you will need to know the account's steamID and most functions will require the profile to be set to public. Your steamID can be found a number of ways, but one of them would be:

  1. Open the steam application
  2. Navigate to the tab labeled with the profile's name
  3. Click to copy the URL to your clipboard
  4. Enter the URL into a tool that can convert it into a steamID such as steamid.io
